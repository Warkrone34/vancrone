# 3. GDPR/KVKK Notice & Disclaimer

> Last Update: 11 September 2026.

## Purpose of This Document

This Disclosure Text informs you, as required by Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and, where applicable, Articles 13–14 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), about how Developer processes your personal data in connection with the Vancrone application.

## 1. Identity of the Data Controller

Data Controller: the independent developer of the Vancrone application, referred to here as the Developer. The publisher details verified by Google are shown on the Vancrone listing on Google Play.

Contact: vancrone.app@gmail.com

Scope of responsibility: The Developer is a data controller only for the limited diagnostic, advertising, and purchase-validation data described below. Your warranty records, invoices, and photos stay on your own device and, if you enable backup, inside your own Google Drive; the Developer has no access to them and is not their controller.

## 2. What Personal Data We Process, and Why

Vancrone's inventory, invoice, and financial-tracking data ("User Content") is processed locally on your device to provide the App's core functionality to you. The Developer does not receive or process this User Content.

The personal data categories that are transmitted to the Developer or its service providers, and why:

- Crash diagnostics: Processed by Google (Firebase Crashlytics) to detect and fix app stability issues.
- Advertising data: Processed by Google (AdMob) to serve and measure ads for free-tier users.
- Purchase data: Processed by Google (Play Billing) for license validation. The Developer does not operate any server for this purpose and only receives the purchase status reported by Google Play.

## 3. Method of Collection and Legal Basis

Method of collection: The data above is collected automatically through the Google SDKs embedded in the App (Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob, Google Play Billing) as you use the App.

Legal basis under KVKK and GDPR: Crash diagnostics and purchase validation are processed on the basis of legitimate interests (keeping the App functional, secure, and fulfilling purchases), provided this does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms, and/or performance of a contract. Baseline (non-personalized) advertising may rely on the same basis; where personalized advertising requires it, we rely on your explicit consent.

## 4. To Whom Your Data May Be Transferred, and Why

Your data may be transferred to Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC and their affiliates, acting as our data processors for Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob, Google Play Billing, and (if you opt in) Google Drive appDataFolder backups. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not transfer your User Content to anyone — because we never receive it in the first place.

## 5. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Some recipients listed in Section 4 (in particular, Google) may process data outside Türkiye and/or outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers are made on the basis of an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses, or another mechanism recognized under Chapter V of the GDPR or Article 9 of KVKK, as implemented by our processors.

## 6. Retention Period

- Your User Content, stored locally on your device, is retained until you delete it, use "Clear All Data," or uninstall the App.
- Crash diagnostics are retained by Google/Firebase according to its standard Crashlytics retention periods.
- Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own advertising-data retention practices.
- Purchase/license data is retained for as long as necessary to support your purchase.

## 7. Your Rights

Under KVKK (Article 11) and GDPR (Articles 15-21), as a data subject, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is being processed, request information, request correction or erasure, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. If you are located in the EU/EEA/UK, you additionally have the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

## 8. How to Exercise Your Rights

You can exercise the rights above by writing to us. We aim to respond to KVKK-based requests as soon as possible and no later than 30 days, and to GDPR-based requests within one month.

## 9. A Note on "Disclosure" vs. "Consent"

This Disclosure Text fulfills our obligation to inform you about how your data is processed — it is not, by itself, a request for your consent. Where we need your separate, explicit consent — for example, for personalized advertising — that consent is requested independently.

## 10. Where to Complain, by Region

- Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurumu).
- European Economic Area: the data protection supervisory authority of the country where you live.
- United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner's Office.
- Switzerland: the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.
- Japan: the Personal Information Protection Commission.
- Other countries: your local data protection or consumer protection authority.

In every case you can write to vancrone.app@gmail.com first, and the Developer will answer within the time limits described in Section 8.
