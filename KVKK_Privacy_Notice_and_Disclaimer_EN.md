# Privacy Notice, KVKK Disclosure, and Disclaimer of Liability

> This document is the comprehensive English disclosure governing the data processing practices and liability limitations of the Vancrone application. Last updated: October 15, 2026.

## Purpose of this Document

This Privacy Notice is provided in accordance with Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), Articles 13–14 of the European Union General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), and applicable global privacy regulations to inform you about how personal data is processed in connection with the Vancrone mobile application.

## 1. Identity of the Data Controller and Scope of Responsibility

Data Controller: The sole independent developer of the Vancrone application, referred to herein as the "Developer". Verified publisher details are displayed on Vancrone's official Google Play store listing.

Contact: vancrone.app@gmail.com

Scope of Responsibility: The Developer is the data controller strictly and exclusively for the limited data described below: crash reports, in-app purchase records (if purchases are introduced), and messages you send through the in-app feedback form. Your warranty records, invoice scans, vault files, and captured photos are stored solely and exclusively in your device's local offline storage. Any encrypted backup files (.vcb) created via the Storage Access Framework (SAF) remain exclusively in the directory selected by you. The Developer operates no user accounts, no cloud databases, and no remote servers for this content, has no access to it, and is NOT the data controller for your local user content.

## 2. What Personal Data We Process and Why

Vancrone’s inventory, invoice, warranty, and financial tracking records ("User Content") are processed locally on your device to provide you with offline-first core functionality. The Developer does not receive, remotely store, or process this User Content.

The limited categories of data processed in connection with the app are:

- Crash Diagnostics (Google Firebase Crashlytics): Stack trace, device model, Android version, app version, a random installation identifier, and the time of the error, used to diagnose and fix stability bugs. This data is pseudonymous: it is not linked to your name or email address and contains no User Content.
- Purchase and License Data (Google Play Billing): The current version offers no in-app purchases and does not connect to Google Play Billing, so no purchase data is processed. If optional Pro/Business upgrades are introduced, order and purchase status information will be processed to deliver the features you buy; payment details will be handled only by Google, and this Notice will be updated beforehand.
- Feedback Messages (only when you send one): Your message, the email address you send it from, device manufacturer and model, Android version, app version, and time of sending, used to answer you and fix the reported issue.

The current version of the app does not display ads and does not include the Google AdMob SDK. If advertising is introduced, this Notice will be updated before that release.

## 3. Collection Method and Legal Basis

Collection Method: Crash reports are collected automatically from your device during app usage via the integrated Firebase Crashlytics library (and, if purchases are introduced, purchase data via Google Play Billing). Feedback messages are received only when you choose to send an email from your own email app to the Developer's address (vancrone.app@gmail.com); the app itself does not transmit them.

Legal Basis under GDPR / KVKK: Crash diagnostics are processed on the legal basis of legitimate interest (maintaining an operational, secure, and bug-free application), provided that this does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms. Purchase data is processed for the performance of a contract (delivering purchased digital features). Feedback messages are processed on the basis of legitimate interest in answering the request you initiated. If personalized advertising is ever introduced, it will rely on your explicit consent.

## 4. Data Transfers and Recipients

Your data may be transferred to Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC and their affiliates acting as data processors for Firebase Crashlytics, Google Play Billing, and Gmail, which hosts the Developer's support mailbox. The application features no Google Drive API synchronization and does not send your User Content to any server. We do not sell your personal data, and we never transfer your User Content to any third party — because we never receive it in the first place.

## 5. Cross-Border Data Transfers

The recipients identified in Section 4 (particularly Google's infrastructure) may process crash, purchase, and feedback data outside your country of residence, including within the United States. Such transfers are conducted pursuant to Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), adequacy decisions, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where applicable, or other lawful transfer mechanisms recognized under GDPR Chapter V and KVKK Article 9.

## 6. Retention Periods

- User Content stored locally on your device is retained until you delete it, use the "Clear All Data" option in Settings, or uninstall the Application.
- Crash diagnostics are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days.
- Purchase/license records are retained by Google via your Google Play account as long as necessary to maintain your license entitlements.
- Feedback messages are kept only as long as needed to answer you and resolve the reported issue.

## 7. Your Rights and Direct User Controls

Under KVKK (Article 11), GDPR (Articles 15–21), and comparable privacy laws, you possess the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, request information, request rectification or erasure, and object to processing based on legitimate interests.

CRITICAL NOTICE: Because Vancrone operates offline without user accounts or central databases, complete direct control over your User Content (inventory, receipts, images) rests in your own hands. You may delete individual records at any time within the app or permanently delete all warranty records, their attachments, and images saved with the Thermal Receipt Restorer via Settings > "Clear All Data". You can turn off crash reporting at any time via Settings > About > "Crash Reports", the most direct way to object to this processing. While the switch is off, no crash reports are sent and reports still waiting on your device are deleted; reports sent before that are retained for the period in Section 6. The current version does not collect your Advertising ID.

## 8. How to Exercise Your Rights and Response Expectations

Vancrone is designed, maintained, and operated by a solo independent developer without a corporate call center or full-time customer support staff.

For questions regarding your statutory rights, you may reach out to vancrone.app@gmail.com. Please note that because the Developer possesses no technical access to your local User Content, the Developer cannot remotely inspect, alter, delete, or recover your local records or lost backups. Because crash reports are pseudonymous, the Developer may not be able to identify which entries relate to you. The Developer aims to review inquiries in good faith within reasonable timeframes based on personal availability and development workload; however, please understand that response times may experience substantial delays during active development periods.

## 9. Distinction Between "Notice" and "Consent"

This Notice fulfills our legal duty to inform you of how data is processed — it does not itself constitute a consent request. The current version of the app does not process data on the basis of consent. If a processing activity that requires your explicit consent is introduced (such as personalized advertising), consent will be requested separately in the app before that processing starts.

## 10. Supervisory Authorities

You retain the statutory right to lodge a complaint with your competent supervisory authority:

- Turkey: Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurumu).
- European Economic Area (EEA): The competent Data Protection Authority (DPA) of your country of residence.
- United Kingdom: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
- Switzerland: Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
- Japan: Personal Information Protection Commission (PPC).
- Other Jurisdictions: Your local national privacy or consumer protection agency.

In all instances, you may first contact vancrone.app@gmail.com for preliminary inquiries.
