# Privacy Notice, KVKK Disclosure, and Disclaimer of Liability

> This document is the comprehensive English disclosure governing the data processing practices and liability limitations of the Vancrone application. Last updated: September 11, 2026.

## Purpose of this Document

This Privacy Notice is provided in accordance with Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), Articles 13–14 of the European Union General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), and applicable global privacy regulations to inform you about how personal data is processed in connection with the Vancrone mobile application.

## 1. Identity of the Data Controller and Scope of Responsibility

Data Controller: The sole independent developer of the Vancrone application, referred to herein as the "Developer". Verified publisher details are displayed on Vancrone's official Google Play store listing.

Contact: vancrone.app@gmail.com

Scope of Responsibility: The Developer is the data controller strictly and exclusively for the limited technical diagnostics, anonymous crash reports, advertising delivery, and in-app purchase verification data described below. Your warranty records, invoice scans, vault files, and captured photos are stored solely and exclusively in your device's local offline storage. Any encrypted backup files (.vcb) created via the Storage Access Framework (SAF) remain exclusively in the directory selected by you. The Developer operates no user accounts, no cloud databases, and no remote servers, has no access to this content, and is NOT the data controller for your local user content.

## 2. What Personal Data We Process and Why

Vancrone’s inventory, invoice, warranty, and financial tracking records ("User Content") are processed locally on your device to provide you with offline-first core functionality. The Developer does not receive, remotely store, or process this User Content.

The limited categories of data automatically transmitted to third-party Google SDKs to enable app operations are:

- Crash Diagnostics: Processed anonymously by Google (Firebase Crashlytics) to diagnose stability bugs and improve app performance.
- Advertising Data: Processed by Google (AdMob) to serve and measure ads for free tier users.
- Purchase and License Data: Processed by Google (Google Play Billing) to verify digital license status. The Developer maintains no licensing servers and only verifies the transaction status reported by Google Play to the device.

## 3. Collection Method and Legal Basis

Collection Method: The data specified above is collected automatically from your device during app usage via integrated Google libraries (Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob, Google Play Billing).

Legal Basis under GDPR / KVKK: Crash diagnostics and purchase verification are processed on the legal basis of legitimate interest (maintaining an operational, secure, and bug-free application) and/or performance of a contract (delivering purchased digital features). Basic (non-personalized) advertising may rely on legitimate interests; where personalized advertising is requested, processing relies on your explicit consent in accordance with Google User Consent Policy.

## 4. Data Transfers and Recipients

Your data may be transferred to Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC and their affiliates acting as data processors for Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob, and Google Play Billing. The application features no Google Drive API synchronization and communicates with no external developer-owned cloud servers. We do not sell your personal data, and we never transfer your User Content to any third party — because we never receive it in the first place.

## 5. Cross-Border Data Transfers

The recipients identified in Section 4 (particularly Google's infrastructure) may process diagnostic and advertising data outside your country of residence, including within the United States. Such transfers are conducted pursuant to Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), adequacy decisions, or other lawful transfer mechanisms recognized under GDPR Chapter V and KVKK Article 9.

## 6. Retention Periods

- User Content stored locally on your device is retained until you delete it, use the "Clear All Data" option in Settings, or uninstall the Application.
- Crash diagnostics are retained by Google/Firebase in accordance with standard Crashlytics retention schedules.
- Advertising data is retained by Google in accordance with its advertising data retention practices.
- Purchase/license records are retained by Google via your Google Play account as long as necessary to maintain your license entitlements.

## 7. Your Rights and Direct User Controls

Under KVKK (Article 11), GDPR (Articles 15–21), and comparable privacy laws, you possess the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, request information, and request rectification or erasure.

CRITICAL NOTICE: Because Vancrone operates offline without user accounts or central databases, complete direct control over your User Content (inventory, receipts, images) rests in your own hands. You may delete individual records at any time within the app or permanently purge all data via Settings > "Clear All Data". You may also reset or disable your Advertising ID at any time via Android Device Settings > Google > Ads.

## 8. How to Exercise Your Rights and Response Expectations

Vancrone is designed, maintained, and operated by a solo independent developer without a corporate call center or full-time customer support staff.

For questions regarding your statutory rights, you may reach out to vancrone.app@gmail.com. Please note that because the Developer possesses no technical access to your local User Content, the Developer cannot remotely inspect, alter, delete, or recover your local records or lost backups. The Developer aims to review inquiries in good faith within reasonable timeframes based on personal availability and development workload; however, please understand that response times may experience substantial delays during active development periods.

## 9. Distinction Between "Notice" and "Consent"

This Notice fulfills our legal duty to inform you of how data is processed — it does not itself constitute a consent request. Where your explicit consent is legally required (such as for personalized advertising), consent is requested independently via dedicated in-app dialogs.

## 10. Supervisory Authorities

You retain the statutory right to lodge a complaint with your competent supervisory authority:

- United Kingdom: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
- Switzerland: Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

In all instances, you may first contact vancrone.app@gmail.com for preliminary inquiries.
